# Level 2 — Reading Trend & Structure Shifts

## What this level covers
Building on Level 1: how to tell when a trend is continuing, weakening, or
actually reversing — before it's obvious.

## The core idea
A trend stays intact as long as it keeps making higher highs and higher
lows (or lower highs/lower lows in a downtrend). The moment that pattern
breaks — a "structure shift" — is the market's earliest signal that
something is changing, well before price confirms it any other way.

## Key concepts to teach
- **Break of structure (BOS)**: price breaks a prior swing high/low in the
  direction of the existing trend — this confirms the trend is continuing.
- **Change of character (CHoCH)**: price breaks a swing point *against*
  the existing trend for the first time — an early warning the trend may
  be turning.
- **Liquidity**: clusters of stop-losses / pending orders sitting just
  beyond obvious highs and lows, which price is often drawn toward before
  reversing ("stop hunts" or "liquidity grabs").
- **Higher-timeframe bias**: checking a higher timeframe's structure
  before trading a lower one, so you're not fighting the bigger trend.

## Common beginner mistakes
- Calling every CHoCH a full reversal — it's a warning sign, not
  confirmation. Many CHoCHs fail and the original trend resumes.
- Ignoring the higher timeframe and trading a reversal that's really just
  a small pullback in a much bigger trend.
- Reacting to a single wick instead of waiting for a candle to actually
  close beyond the structure point.

## Example to walk through
Show an uptrend that makes a lower high for the first time (an early
CHoCH signal), pull back further than prior pullbacks, then either
resume the uptrend (failed CHoCH) or break the last higher low too
(confirmed reversal). Discuss how a student would have read each stage.

## What a student must demonstrate to pass this level
Given a chart, correctly identify the most recent BOS or CHoCH, state
what it implies about the trend, and explain what would need to happen
next to confirm or invalidate that read.
